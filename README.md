# SoulSeesBest
## The Best Band in the Universe ✨

SoulSeesBest is a multi-genre, symphonic, intelligent Electronica experience, meticulously crafted of soul, orchestral, urban, Latinx, jazz, downtempo and world influences, with universal ambitions, rooted in Ancient Earth.

## Music
SoulSeesBest is an electronic instrumental project based in Southern California, Arizona, and New Mexico. Primary tools include GarageBand for iOS and MacOS, Casio CTK-2000, AudioKit digital drum sequencers and synthesizers, and various live instrumentation and custom sound effects. 

The band is currently streaming on all major platforms and at the official website.  
https://soulsees.best

## App
This repo is being managed and 'vibe coded' by @WriterJoshua, with the help of Muse AI, to create a customized, exclusive "radio broadcast" player for SoulSeesBest. 

### Broadcast
It is built to host long playing audio presentations, with several hosting options being tested including: GitHub, WordPress, RSS and others. It includes a "fake-live" feature that recognizes a 'broadcast schedule' so that, when a user tunes in, the audio starts playback based on the current time of the device accessing it. Anyone, anywhere, tuning in will hear the same piece of media at the same time, by loading the specific timestamp relative to defined 'broadcast schedule.' This gives the appearance of tuning into a live broadcast. Users are then encouraged to download the pop-out window as a PWA to act as a free, 24 hour broadcast, completely free and managed by @WriterJoshua for SoulSeesBest.

## Goals
The intention of this repo is to develop an official SoulSeesBest Fan Club App, with the key feature being the broadcast element.
